# RSI v6.2 — Understand and apply AI improvement cycles

A free course in Portuguese and English: 18 lessons, six modules, 54 review cards, a glossary and a supervised final project.

- [Study in English](https://inematds.github.io/curso-rsi/en/)
- [Estudar em português](https://inematds.github.io/curso-rsi/)
- [RSI topic area](https://eventos.inema.pro/rsi/en/)
- [English practice materials](en/materiais/)

The examples are fictional. The exercises teach supervised improvement; they do not demonstrate autonomous recursive self-improvement. Original sources accompany the lessons. The full editorial research report remains in Portuguese and is labelled accordingly.

Progress is stored in your browser separately for each language. The menu supports export and import. No account is required. There is no Spanish course edition.

Validation includes automated structural checks, browser tests and simulated reader review; no study with real learners was conducted.
